# Measurement scenario for layout reuse, not golden-gated: the three input
# triggers that can relay a page out, measured separately on a loaded
# page. Each mark closes one trigger's window, so the tilefinch-layout-diff and
# tilefinch-geometry-adoption lines between two marks belong to it.
# R: three ready-gated page presses.
# hover: the nub cursor sweeps left and right across the content (about
#   7 px a frame at full deflection), sending a pointer move every frame.
# edge: the cursor runs to the bottom edge and the held nub scrolls the
#   page from there (about 26-40 px a frame), the analog scroll path.
wait-live 90
wait-page 90
mark-page top
tap-page rtrigger
wait-page 45
tap-page rtrigger
wait-page 45
tap-page rtrigger
wait-page 45
mark-page r3
stick 30 left
stick 30 right
stick 30 right
stick 30 left
wait-page 45
mark-page hover
stick 25 down
stick 90 down
wait-page 45
mark-page edge
end
