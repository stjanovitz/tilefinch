# Page audio from a loopback station (a page with one <audio>, started with
# --url http://127.0.0.1:PORT/...): focus it, play it, listen for about
# twenty seconds, then close the player. A measurement scenario: the log
# carries the MP3 open, the decode counters and any failure.
wait-live 1200
wait-page 300
mark-page loaded
focus-page-media
tap cross
until-live 1350
mark-live playing
wait-live 1200
mark-live listened
tap-live circle
wait-live 150
mark closed
end
