# DEVICE-LOOP SCRIPT -- NOT A GOLDEN-GATED SCENARIO.
#
# A YouTube music video on real hardware: play, seek forward, then rewind far
# enough to rebuild the decoder over the network while presses arrive. Run
# through the PSPLink loop with boot.cfg
#   url=https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=m7k9UMcHbr0   (KATSEYE "Animal")
#   input_script=youtube-seek-rewind-play.txt
# In order: play 20 s; three 10 s previews committed with Cross; a rewind to 0
# (backward-seek-reopen) with Right and Cross pressed while the reopen runs
# (expected to land near 10 s, playing); play on; close. Read the
# tilefinch-media-* lines and the tilefinch-input-script-media marks. The
# network decides the timings, so nothing is gated. No screenshot step.
wait 1
mark-live watch
tap-live cross
wait-live 1500
mark-live playing
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live right
wait-live 30
tap-live cross
wait-live 900
mark-live committed
tap-live ltrigger
wait-live 30
tap-live cross
wait-live 30
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live cross
wait-live 1500
mark-live after-rewind
tap-live circle
wait-live 180
mark closed
end
