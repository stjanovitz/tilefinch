# Internet radio from directory.shoutcast.com on a real PSP: play the first
# MP3 station in the Top list with the d-pad and, when the station's HTTPS
# upgrade fails, answer the unencrypted-audio question with Cross. Boot with
# url=https://directory.shoutcast.com/.
#
# Down walks the header (menu, logo, search, Advanced Search, the cookie
# notice's privacy link) to the first station's name; Left reaches its play
# link. The companion mark.js reports the focused element and station at
# "focused". From the play press on the player is a sustained media session,
# so every later step is -live: an ordinary step would wait for an idle
# browser that never comes. answer-question presses Cross only if the
# question appears (logging outcome=answered and capturing "question"); a
# station whose upgrade succeeds plays at once (outcome=not-asked).
until 2400
tap down
wait 8
tap down
wait 8
tap down
wait 8
tap down
wait 8
tap down
wait 8
tap down
wait 30
tap left
wait 30
mark focused
tap cross
answer-question-live 2400 cross
wait-live 900
mark-live playing
wait-live 3600
mark-live listened
tap-live circle
wait-live 120
mark-live closed
wait 30
end
