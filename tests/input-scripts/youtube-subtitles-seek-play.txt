# DEVICE-LOOP SCRIPT -- NOT A GOLDEN-GATED SCENARIO.
#
# A long YouTube video with subtitles on real hardware (PPSSPP stops at the
# firmware decoder boundary). Run through the PSPLink loop with boot.cfg
#   url=https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=IoDVfXFq5cU   (MrBeast, many caption
#                                                   tracks and dubbed audio)
#   input_script=youtube-subtitles-seek-play.txt
# In order: play; open the track menu with Triangle, switch to the Subtitles
# tab (the menu opens on Audio when the video has dubs), pick the first
# track; play on with captions; three 10 s previews committed with Cross;
# then a rewind to 0 (large enough to rebuild the decoder over the network)
# with Right and Cross pressed while that reopen runs. Read
# tilefinch-media-first-frame, tilefinch-subtitle*, tilefinch-media-job
# seek-complete/backward-seek-reopen/resume-open, and the
# tilefinch-input-script-media marks. The network decides the timings, so
# nothing is gated. No screenshot step: that writes to the Memory Stick.
wait 1
mark-live watch
tap-live cross
wait-live 1500
mark-live playing
tap-live triangle
wait-live 30
tap-live rtrigger
wait-live 20
tap-live down
wait-live 20
tap-live cross
wait-live 900
mark-live subtitles
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live right
wait-live 30
tap-live cross
wait-live 900
mark-live committed
tap-live ltrigger
wait-live 30
tap-live cross
wait-live 30
tap-live right
wait-live 10
tap-live cross
wait-live 1500
mark-live after-rewind
tap-live circle
wait-live 180
mark closed
end
